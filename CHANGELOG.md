# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-29)

First public release.

- A 12-hour clock with the date and three world clocks, each with US, EU, Australian, New Zealand or no daylight
  saving.
- LTA DataMall bus arrivals for one stop (up to five services) on a bus board, along the bottom in the morning, and
  as a bus that pulls up when one is a minute out.
- NEA's 2-hour forecast as ten animated weather scenes, outside temperature, humidity and feels-like, UV, PSI, and a
  24-hour and 4-day forecast.
- A sourced Wikiquote line every 30 minutes, with 49 built-in quotes until the first one arrives.
- Indoor temperature and humidity with a 24-hour chart, and battery level.
- A 25-minute focus timer that grows a permanent garden, a daily intention sent over USB, and a photo frame for
  quiet evenings with nature photos and your own dated postcards.
- A cat companion with offline voice commands (ESP-SR), renamed with one setting.
- Setup from a phone, every personal choice in `config.h`, pinned builds with `sketch.yaml`, and self-healing for
  Wi-Fi, stale feeds and hangs.
